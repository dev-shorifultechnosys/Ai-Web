ABZ INNOVATION C10
THE SYSTEM

Built for professional cleaning operations.

The ABZ Innovation C10 delivers high-pressure cleaning at height while keeping the operating team on the ground. The system supports working heights over 60 metres, flow rates up to 15 litres per minute and working pressure around 170 bar at the nozzle, with up to 255 bar at peak.

Radar collision avoidance and standoff control support stable working distance. Reverse osmosis water can also be used where appropriate.

UK / SPECIFIC CATEGORYOperating legally.

Operations of this type sit within the Specific Category and require an Operational Authorisation from the CAA. Since April 2025, this has been through UK SORA. The authorisation is held by the operator, not the supplier.

We assist clients through the process. Our own PowerWash cleaning operation received its UK SORA authorisation in January 2026. The first client Operational Authorisation for the C10 has been granted in the client’s name, with two more at final assessment.
